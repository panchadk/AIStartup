Notes (about 10 minutes)

  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) retrieves relevant passages first, then gives them to a language model as context for its answer.
  • RAG lets a model use private or fresh data without retraining, and it reduces hallucination.
  • A good prompt tells the model to answer only from the provided passages, to cite them, and to say "I don't know" when the answer is not there.
  • Retrieval comes first. If the right passage is not retrieved, the model cannot answer correctly.
  • Cost scales with the number of tokens sent and generated. The retrieved passages are most of what you send.
  • Test with questions the documents cannot answer, not just the ones they can.

  7. Run the test. At least 80% correct, with a source for every answer.
  8. Cost. Write cost_estimate() and work out the cost per question.
  9. Stretch: log the cost per answer, add conversation memory, or compare models.
